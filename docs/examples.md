# Examples

These example animations show different parts of animating with smoothstep. You can also open
them from File → Open in the app, or from Help → Welcome, where each one has a moving preview.
If you change an example, save it to keep it as one of your own animations.

## Showcase

* [Wobbling mirror ball](https://smoothstep.io/#example/c6e454d960df): a mirror ball, pulled into
  the shapes of spherical harmonics and left to spring back, reflecting a 360° photo.
* [Menger sponge dissection](https://smoothstep.io/#example/2ed11c16d844): a Menger sponge, sliced
  open by a moving plane.
* [Julia ring](https://smoothstep.io/#example/be71615e6614): a ring whose slices are the Julia
  sets along the edge of the Mandelbrot set's main cardioid, cut open to show them.
* [Domain warping](https://smoothstep.io/#example/19bb8790cb0d): fractal noise fed back into
  itself, like marbled paper.
* [Kaleidoscope](https://smoothstep.io/#example/d692b3fe2561): a painting, mirrored into turning
  segments with polar coordinates.
* [Glowing trails](https://smoothstep.io/#example/3156da0acaeb): dots that leave fading trails,
  drawn on top of the previous frame.
* [Pokémon type battles](https://smoothstep.io/#example/65ca463c0318): cells of the 18 Pokémon
  types fight their neighbours, and the type chart decides who wins. The Canvas2D layer draws the legend.
* [Hypocycloids](https://smoothstep.io/#example/96abdd1ee3d3): circles rolling inside and outside a
  circle, tracing curves with the Canvas2D layer's drawing helpers.
* [Bean machine](https://smoothstep.io/#example/7852e2820a47): balls bounce down through pegs and
  pile up in a bell curve, simulated with planck.js in the Canvas2D layer.
* [Hexagonal growth](https://smoothstep.io/#example/db5ea9765f9f): one hexagon grows into a
  snowflake, as each empty cell with exactly one living neighbour comes alive.
* [Truchet tiles](https://smoothstep.io/#example/bd2cdbfe233d): tiles that turn smoothly, rejoining
  into winding paths.
* [Metaballs](https://smoothstep.io/#example/3d6489cf0694): blobs that melt into each other and
  pull apart, following the timeline.
